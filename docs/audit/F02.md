# F02 — Runtime/doctor sibling identity agreement

Execution and doctor now share validation of local sibling roots and every present working/committed Build.toml. Missing legacy manifests remain allowed with a doctor warning. Present contradictory or malformed manifests fail. A working-tree edit cannot mask a contradictory committed identity, because local sibling content comes from commits. Local fetched commits are validated again before merge to cover a changed checkout between resolution and fetching.

Remote URLs are not constrained to canonical organizations: legitimate forks remain supported. Remote-network sibling policy is unchanged. Tests cover working/committed disagreement, correct fork identity, transitional missing manifests, malformed manifests and parent-repository discovery. Executable acceptance exercises build and doctor with the original WrongRepo fixture and then correct/legacy controls.
